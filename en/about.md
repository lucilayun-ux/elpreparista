## What El Preparista is

The blackout of 28 April 2025 in Spain showed that many people did not even have a battery torch at home. Putting together an emergency kit is not hard, but it takes time: knowing what you need, how much, and which specific product to buy among hundreds.

We do that work for you. You choose the kit (home, car or evacuation), the level and how many of you there are, and we leave the list ready to buy on Amazon.

## How we choose each product

1. **We start from official recommendations.** Each kit follows what the European Union (72-hour self-sufficiency), Spanish Civil Protection and the DGT (for the car) recommend.
2. **It has to work without power.** Standard battery torches, hand-crank or solar radios, power banks you charge before the outage. Nothing that depends on the socket when you need it most.
3. **It has to last.** Emergency water and food with up to 5 years of shelf life, so you do not have to replace them all the time.
4. **Good ratings and plenty of reviews.** We choose products with high ratings and a large number of buyer reviews on Amazon Spain, not simply the cheapest or the best-selling.
5. **Calculated quantities.** Water, food and blankets are adjusted to the number of people.
6. **Always available.** If a product sells out, we replace it with an equivalent alternative so your list arrives complete.

Next to each product in the list we explain in one line **why that one and not another**.

## How we make money

The products are bought on Amazon. We are Amazon affiliates: if you buy through our links, **Amazon pays us a small commission and you pay the same**. That is what keeps the website, the expiry reminders and the free guide going.

We do not sell anything directly, we do not store your payment details, and the purchase, delivery and returns are with Amazon, like any other order of yours.

## When we review the products

We review the lists regularly: if a product sells out, its ratings drop or a better option appears, we change it. Blog articles show when they were published and when they were updated.

## What we are not

We are not an official emergency service. In an emergency, call **112** and always follow the authorities' instructions.
